Skinoz is for players who love collecting and trading in-game skins. Everything happens inside Telegram or the browser: open the app and you are ready to play, with no downloads and no complicated sign-up. The platform supports skins from CS2, Dota 2 and Rust, is available in many languages, and uses crystals, a simple internal currency, for everything you do. Every skin you get is stored in your personal inventory, and you can withdraw it to your Steam account whenever you like.

There are plenty of ways to play. Open cases from a large, regularly updated collection, or face other players in Case Battles, where the best drop wins everything. Use the Upgrader to try turning a skin into a more valuable one, or put several items into a Contract to get something new. If you like competing live, join Battle Royale, Double, Wheel 50 or Crash, where rounds play out in real time for all players at once. If you already know which skin you want, you can buy it directly in the Market.

Skinoz is also built around its community. Join a gang or start your own, chat with your teammates, compete in daily gang rankings and capture game modes to fill your team's treasury. Grow your level, unlock achievements and make your public profile your own with custom colors, a background and a showcase of your best drops. Surprise Rush Drop events, seasonal giveaways, promo codes and a referral program give you more reasons to come back every day.
